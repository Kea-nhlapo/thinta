# Thinta + Fika pitch (3 minutes)

Times are where you should be. Lines in [brackets] are what you do on the phone, not what you say.

---

**0:00 Hi**

Hi everyone, I'm Kea. So this is SA Educate, our app that turns a teacher's PDF into a lesson any learner can follow. Tonight we added Thinta. Thinta means touch.

**0:15 The problem**

So here's the problem. Maths is full of graphs, and blind and Deaf learners can't use them. In 2015 only 2 out of 22 schools for blind learners even offered maths. And if a blind learner needs a graph they can feel, someone has to redraw it by hand. That takes weeks.

So we built a team of agents that does it in about a minute. Let me show you.

[Take the photo of the parabola. Tap Start the team.]

**0:40 Agents that read it and check it**

I just took a photo of a graph. Our first agents read the photo and pull out the equation and the points. Then the Prover checks the maths with algebra.

Why? Because AI is bad at reading charts. It gets about 63% right. People get 93. So we never trust one read. If the reader gets something wrong, you see it crossed out and fixed.

[Point at the strike-through, or the "What the team caught" cards.]

**1:05 Agents that make a version for each learner**

Then the next agents make a version for each learner. For a blind learner, there's a print they can feel, with braille on it. And they can drag a finger across the screen and hear the graph go up and down.

[Drag a finger across the graph.]

They can also listen to a description, in English, isiZulu or Sepedi. For a Deaf learner, there's a step-by-step answer with a picture for every step.

**1:35 The agent that checks everything**

Then there's the Reviewer, and this is my favourite one. It reads the print without seeing the photo. Then it tries to answer the worksheet using only the description. If it can't, it sends the work back to be fixed. In one of our runs the Deaf solution got 0 out of 5, got rewritten, and came back 5 out of 5.

And nothing goes to a learner until the teacher says yes.

[Teacher tab. Tap Approve the pack.]

**2:05 Fika gets it to every learner**

Once the teacher approves, Fika takes over. Fika means arrive. These agents make sure every learner gets their pack. Phone packs go out straight away, so learners can start today. The prints get booked on the school's machines.

Watch what happens if the embosser breaks.

[Delivery tab. Tap Take embosser offline.]

Fika moves everything to another machine. The old time gets crossed out and the new time shows up. And if a learner can't get it in time, it tells the teacher.

**2:45 Close**

So a blind learner gets the graph today, by touch and sound, and the print follows. That's Thinta. Thank you.

---

## If something goes wrong on stage

- **No strike-through because the Reader got it right:** "This time it read it right, so the Prover just confirms it. When it's wrong, you see it crossed out."
- **The Wi-Fi is slow or drops:** tick Replay. "The Wi-Fi's slow, so this is a recorded real run. You can see it says Replay at the top."
- **The isiZulu or Sepedi doesn't show:** "If the translation changes a number or a time, Thinta holds it back. So here it's in English."
- **Running long:** drop the 63% line. Never drop the Reviewer bit.
