# Thinta + Fika pitch (3 minutes)

Times are where you should be. Lines in [brackets] are what you do on the phone, not what you say.

---

**0:00 SA Educate**

Hi, I'm Kea. SA Educate is our app that turns a teacher's PDF into a lesson every learner can follow, including in sign language. Tonight we added Thinta. It's isiZulu for touch.

**0:15 The problem**

Maths lives in graphs, and blind and Deaf learners get locked out of them. In 2015, only 2 of 22 schools for blind learners offered maths. A tactile copy of one graph can take weeks, because someone redraws it by hand.

Melsoft asked for a colleague. Thinta is a team of agents that does that job. A teacher takes a photo of a graph.

[Take the photo of the parabola. Tap Start the team.]

**0:40 The first group reads and proves**

The first group works out what the graph really says. The Reader pulls the equation and points off the photo. Then the Prover does the maths with algebra.

That matters because the best AI model scores 63% on reading charts, and people score 93. So we never trust one read. When the Reader gets it wrong, you see its answer crossed out and the Prover's put in.

[Point at the strike-through.]

**1:05 The second group builds a version for every learner**

The next group turns that one graph into something each learner can use. The Designer draws a tactile print with braille labels for swell paper. The Sonifier turns the curve into sound, so a blind learner drags a finger across and hears it.

[Drag a finger across the graph.]

The Explainer writes an audio description in English and isiZulu, and a step-by-step solution for Deaf learners, with a picture for each step.

**1:35 The Reviewer checks everything**

The last agent is why I'd trust this. The Reviewer reads the tactile print without ever seeing the photo. Then it answers the worksheet using only the description. If it can't, the work goes back. In our run, the Deaf solution scored 0 out of 5, then 5 out of 5 after the rewrite.

Nothing reaches a learner until the teacher approves.

[Teacher tab. Tap Approve the pack.]

**2:05 Fika gets it to every learner**

That hands over to Fika, which means arrive. A second team gets the pack to each learner. Phone packs land straight away, so learners start today. Tactile prints get scheduled on the school's machines, and a checker makes sure nothing is overbooked.

Watch what happens if the embosser breaks.

[Delivery tab. Tap Take embosser offline.]

Fika replans. The old time is crossed out and the new one shows. If a deadline really can't be met, it says so and flags it for the teacher.

**2:45 Close**

So a blind learner gets the graph today, by touch and sound, and the print follows. That's Thinta. Thank you.

## If something goes wrong on stage

- **The Reader gets it right, so there's no strike-through:** "This time the Reader read it correctly, so the Prover just confirms it. When it's off, you'll see it crossed out." Then keep going.
- **The network is slow or drops:** tick Replay. "The Wi-Fi's slow, so this is a recorded real run. It says Replay at the top."
- **The isiZulu summary doesn't show:** "Fika holds back a translation if it changes a time or a day, so here you get English." Say it calmly, it's a strength.
- **You're running long:** cut the 63% line. Never cut the Reviewer section.
